# Version 0.8.6

This bilingual revision extends the discussion of the AI-led model-production loop. Section 6.5 explicitly describes the production and improvement of VLA models themselves, distinguishes this from command generation and single-task fine-tuning, and develops the broader vision of a reusable production approach across model families. Section 7 connects this direction to shared robot foundation models and humanoids.

The loop connects behavior-data generation, task design and evaluation, failure analysis, training-data and weight revision, reevaluation, and feedback from human use. The existing experimental results, numerical values, citations, six tables and three figures are unchanged. No new experiments are reported.

The Korean PDF has 21 pages and the English PDF has 17 pages. These PDFs were rendered with LibreOffice; font substitution and pagination differ from the Word-rendered previous release. The revised discussion and conclusion pages were visually checked.

The package contains Korean and English Markdown, DOCX and PDF files, six figure assets, this revision note and SHA256SUMS.txt. CC BY 4.0 applies to the papers, not automatically to linked code, datasets or model weights.
