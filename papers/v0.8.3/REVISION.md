# Manuscript v0.8.3

This bilingual revision integrates a long-term research hypothesis into the introduction and Section 6.3: advances in LLM physical perception and reasoning may support understanding of parameter–behavior relationships, targeted direct weight editing, and purpose-specific model production.

“Weight modification alone” refers to the improvement stage, not elimination of initial demonstrations and training, testing, or real-world feedback. The proposed tests compare predicted behavioral effects, matched-budget controls, and retention of existing capabilities. This is a research agenda, not an ability demonstrated by the reported experiments.
